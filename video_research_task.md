# Task: Find High-Quality Old Event Videos — 2-3 Year Old Archives vs Current

## Background
User asked whether videos uploaded 2-3 years ago (2023-2024) might have better quality than what's available now (2026), for these events:
1. **Princess Diana Funeral** (1997) — TV broadcast, ITN/BBC
2. **Columbine High School Massacre** (1999) — TV news, Denver local stations + networks
3. **JFK Jr. Death** (1999) — TV news, helicopter coverage
4. **Mother Teresa: In My Own Words** (2008) — DVD release

**Theory:** YouTube re-compression degrades quality over time. Digitization projects in 2023-2025 created new higher-quality sources. So 2023-2024 uploads of newly-digitized material could be better than current YouTube re-encodes.

## Research Summary

### Theoretical Basis
- YouTube re-compression degrades quality with each re-upload cycle
- 2023-2025 digitization efforts: Library of Congress, British Pathé (100 years newsreel to YouTube, 2023), UCLA Film Archive, Internet Archive individuals digitizing VHS tapes
- British Pathé 2023 mass upload covers pre-1970s (not 1990s events)

### Findings by Event

#### 1. Princess Diana Funeral (1997)
- **Original masters:** ITN, BBC, Getty Images (licensed)
- **TFTS Archive:** ITV LP recording — poor quality
- **Archive.org finds:**
  - "Diana: A Celebration, 1961-1997 (1997 UK VHS)" — uploaded **March 27, 2024** by Louis' BBC VHS Channel. A 2024 digitization of a 1997 BBC VHS. ✅ 2024 upload of VHS source.
  - "The Funeral Of Princess Diana 6th September 1997 (7 Hours Of News Footage By ITV News)" — 7 hours of ITV news coverage
  - "BBC News Coverage of Princess Diana's Death 9pm 31st August 1997" — uploaded 2017 by VHS Deck
- **Getty Images:** Licensed high-quality ITN pool footage (paid)
- **BBC Parliament:** 2007 rebroadcast of funeral coverage

#### 2. Columbine (1999)
- **Original sources:** Denver local stations (KUSA, KMGH) + network news pools
- **Archive.org finds:**
  - "The Columbine Media Motherlode Has Dropped!" — 230 files by @researcharchive99 — **very recent drop** (2024/2025). Substack: "download everything quickly before files disappear"
  - "Columbine High School Shootings (April 20, 1999)" — VHS Vault, uploaded 2022-10-10
  - "ABC News: The Day It Happened" — compilation, notes full segment on YouTube with "bad VHS artifacts"
  - "The Complete Columbine Archive (zero-day_202203)" — 3GB collection including "Columbine High School shooting news coverage 1999[SD,854x480]"
  - "The Columbine Files" — 3.0GB collection with news videos
- **Notable:** LewRockwell 2024 article references a 4-hour VHS tape of WCVB-TV live coverage

#### 3. JFK Jr. Death (1999)
- **Original sources:** Network news pools, helicopter footage. **No crash footage exists.**
- **Archive.org finds:**
  - "JFK Jr. File" — uploaded 2025-08-15
  - "JFK Jr 1999 Spyder Secrets" — uploaded 2025-05-14
- **Gordon Skene Sound Collection:** CBS Radio reports July 16-17, 1999
- **LewRockwell 2024:** References 4-hour VHS tape of WCVB-TV live coverage

#### 4. Mother Teresa: In My Own Words (2008)
- **DVD release.** Source quality is fixed by DVD mastering. Recency doesn't matter — a DVD rip is a DVD rip. Quality depends on ripper, not upload date. **No recency benefit.**
